# Contribution
NEON, an NSF program, collected these observations. They were processed through
workflows of the kind NMDC uses, which is what the `nmdc_` element of the name
records, and they are hosted in the `kbase` tenant alongside other derived work.

Three separate facts, all worth having: who collected the data, how it was
processed, and where it lives.
